# The note to the testers, build 6

One WhatsApp message to the "Garu Testers" group when build 6 is on the closed track (build 5 was never uploaded; this one carries both). Short, three
things to try, one question. The replies are the feedback Google asked to see; each one goes into
`play-store.md` under "Applying for production" with the change it led to.

## English

> Garu got an update. Open the Play Store, find Garu, tap Update (or wait a day, it updates itself).
>
> New this time: swipe a card right to approve or left to decline, or tap Edit and approve your own
> words; open the app after a few hours and Home tells you what happened while you were away; a run's
> page says why it did what it did; and the faces move.
>
> Three things to try, two minutes:
> 1. Open the app. A "What's new" card shows up; read it and tap Got it.
> 2. Tap Try the demo, then Bea, and open her last run. Tell me if "Why it did that" makes sense.
> 3. In the demo's Inbox, swipe the waiting draft right to approve it, or left to decline.
>
> One question back, please: what was confusing or annoying, even if small? That answer is what gets
> the app approved.

## Español

> Garu tiene una actualización. Abre la Play Store, busca Garu y toca Actualizar (o espera un día, se
> actualiza sola).
>
> Lo nuevo: desliza una tarjeta a la derecha para aprobar o a la izquierda para rechazar, o toca Editar y
> aprueba con tus propias palabras; abre la app tras unas horas e Inicio te cuenta qué pasó mientras no
> estabas; la página de una ejecución dice por qué hizo lo que hizo; y las caras se mueven.
>
> Tres cosas por probar, dos minutos:
> 1. Abre la app. Sale una tarjeta de "Novedades"; léela y toca Entendido.
> 2. Toca Probar la demo, luego Bea, y abre su última ejecución. Dime si "Por qué hizo eso" se entiende.
> 3. En la Bandeja de la demo, desliza el borrador que espera a la derecha para aprobarlo, o a la izquierda para rechazarlo.
>
> Una pregunta de vuelta, por favor: ¿qué te confundió o te molestó, aunque sea pequeño? Esa respuesta
> es lo que hace que aprueben la app.

## Where the replies go

Each reply, in the tester's words, dated, under "Applying for production" in `play-store.md`, with the
change it led to (or "no change: …" and why). Build 6 ships at least one of those changes and its
release notes name it.
